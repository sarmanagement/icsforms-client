# Fictional incident examples

## Maple Ridge: missing adult with dementia, operational period 1

[`maple-ridge-dementia-op1.json`](maple-ridge-dementia-op1.json) is a portable
**fictional, illustrative exercise**, not an operational plan. Every person,
agency, place, contact, medical arrangement and approval is invented. Do not
call its example numbers or use its radio settings as authorization to transmit.
Actual searches require qualified local command, current intelligence, verified
resources, permissions and a locally approved safety/medical/communications plan.

### Open and explore

1. Use **File → Import → Import Incident JSON…** and select the example. Import creates a separate
   incident; it does not replace the current incident.
   External imports are checked strictly. A rejected file opens an errors/warnings
   report with JSON property paths and a **Copy Report** action; the active incident
   remains unchanged. This example is tested to import without errors or warnings.
   At startup or through **File → Open… / Manage Incidents…**, the combined incident
   manager can open or import an incident, start a new one, back up stored JSON, and
   delete inactive incidents; it prevents deleting the active incident.
2. The incident opens in **SAR**, **DURING_OP**, with
   **Operational period active** recorded. The summer operational period is
   July 18, 2026, **0700–1900 America/New_York**. Entries represent an **0815
   exercise snapshot**: later work and reminders are planned, not accomplished.
   JSON timestamps follow the application's UTC storage convention (the
   operational period is stored as 1100–2300 UTC); configured display-timezone
   conversion keeps the forms aligned with the local-time objectives/reminders.
3. Review ICS 202 objectives, the flat organization, primary ICS 204, linked SAR
   tasks, T-cards, ICS 205/205A, human ICS 206 and adapted ICS 206CANINE,
   ICS 215A/208, ICS 214 and ICS 233-CG. **View → ICP Activity Timeline…**
   displays the ICP log in assignment lanes with centered lifecycle/clue markers.
   Select or right-click a row to inspect its events and assigned resources, add
   an ICP log entry for that task, or open its task/assignment form; hover over a
   marker for event details.
   Export individual PDFs or the IAP bundle to inspect their relationships.

### Scenario and deliberate limits

The fictional missing adult has dementia and wandered from the residence/IPP
at **1 Trail Lane**. Staging and the ground ambulance rendezvous are at the
community park pavilion, **20 Park Road, 0.8 km west of IPP**. All six command
staff work at the separate **ICP, county community center, 50 Civic Road,
1.5 km west of IPP**; MEDL coordinates medical requests from ICP rather than
staffing an additional field post. Shared incident locations include the ICP,
staging, residence/IPP (Other), and **Base (supply and rehab) colocated with staging**
at the same **20 Park Road** address. Base provides water resupply, heat rehab
coordination and the human first-aid supplies/cache, **not a staffed medical
team or aid post**. Staff retain their organizational roles and ICP address;
status **ICP** puts them in the **ICP rack column**, not Assigned (status matches
the existing board header before location).
Assigned teams travel from staging on foot or in personal vehicles; no ground
support transport unit is staffed, and teams do not sign in and out at staging
for individual accountability checks.
Assignment team leaders are not duplicated as additional organizational positions;
ICS 207 derives their boxes from the linked assignments.
All addresses are fictional. A Koster **hub/spokes/rim** approach
protects that residence/last-known-point hub, rapidly checks three travel
spokes, observes two likely rim exits, and
follows with bounded area searches and familiar-attractor checks. It does not
claim an impermeable perimeter, a guaranteed detection probability or a real map.

There are **36 human field searchers, including four canine handlers**, plus
**six ICP staff** (IC, Ops, Planning, Safety, COML and MEDL): **42 people and
46 T-cards including four dogs**. IC Avery Wren is Sheriff of the Fictional
Pine County Sheriff's Office. Field cards include Fictional Maple Ridge Volunteer
SAR, Fictional Cedar Valley SAR and Fictional Lake Hollow SAR personnel.
There is no Logistics Chief; Casey Finch leads ground team G1.
Exactly **four canine equipment T-cards** link to named handlers; dogs are not
counted as people. Two nominal eight-person ground teams, two two-person observation teams
and four handler groups account for all 36 field people, with support reassigned
as follows. Every tasked canine team has **one handler and three support searchers**. Teams
report directly to Ops; there are no branches, divisions or groups and no
additional ICS 204s. Two canine launches are deferred to 1030; Ops must reassess
active span and conditions before launch, staggering or pausing work if necessary;
eight simultaneously active teams is not assumed manageable. Seven teams are initially
active (G1-A, G1-B, G2-A, two posts, K1 and K2). G1 splits into **four-person
G1-A (S1, route A, MR-P01–04)** and **four-person G1-B (S2, route B, MR-P05–08)**;
**four-person G2-A (S3, route C, MR-P09–12)** covers the third route. All three
routes run **0715–1000 concurrently**. G2's **MR-P13–15 completed visual tracker
task T1 around IPP 0700–0800**, after their 0630 check-in;
the returned task retains its stable **T1** label and task/assignment identities in the IAP.
They **departed staging at 0700**, were **on task at 0705**, reported a clue at
**0730**, **completed the task and began returning at 0750**, and **returned to
staging at 0800**. Their **0810 debrief**
records an actual **definitely human sign line leading north**, but **no clear
prints or sole pattern** and no verified association with the missing adult.
The potential clue was **flagged on Ops instruction** and is linked to T1 in
the clue log; follow-up is **pending, not yet undertaken at 0815**. Its limited
coverage and observed hazards are recorded, not extrapolated to an area search.
These three trackers are now **Available at staging**
for handler **MR-P29 (Devon Heath)** on planned K3 at 1030; they are actual K3
task members, not merely mentioned in prose. K3's original support **MR-P30–32**
are explicitly released to staging reserve; G2's eighth member **MR-P16** also
remains reserve until 1030. K1, K2 and K4 retain their original memberships.
G1 reassembles its original **eight** for attractor searches 1030–1200 then
grid subsegments 1230–1700. G2's **eight-person grid**, 1030–1700, comprises
MR-P09–12, MR-P16 and released support MR-P30–32; MR-P13–15 stay with K3.
No member has overlapping tasks, including planned tasks.
There are **13 linked task/ICS 204 rows**: one returned tracker task, seven
on-task assignments and five planned tasks. Only the returned task has a
completed debrief and named debrief supervisor. All other tasks have blank
debrief notes, POD, coverage gaps, observed hazards, supervisor, debrief preparer
and debrief timestamp; front-side preparation and planned assignment times remain.
Start/end times are assignment windows, not a claim that unfinished work is
complete; T1's 0700–0800 window includes travel.
ICS 214 records **24 chronological entries**, beginning with **0630 command
established and all 42 people checked in, including six IMT**. Each of the seven
on-task assignments has its own assignment-linked **departed staging** and
**on task** entries: S1/S2/S3 and R1/R2 at **0710/0715**, K1/K2 at **0725/0730**.
The ICP-scoped log identifies **Ops Morgan Vale** as the log keeper and lists all
six overhead staff plus the people and canine resources assigned to each active
or completed task.
T1 has separate departure, on-task, clue, completion/returning, returned and
debrief entries matching the times above. Event types use the model's built-in
identities and resource labels match their linked tasks. Planned tasks have **no
invented actual departure or on-task events**. The final snapshot is at **0815**.
Each canine task assigns **Leader/Handler, Communications, Navigation and
Searcher / flanker** to its four people, and **Live Find K9** to its equipment card.

Measurable objectives address the required responder/public safety wording,
spoke completion by 1000, containment of both likely egress points by 0800
**through 1900**, searching the high-probability inner **25% zone** to coverage
approximately **1 by 1700**, and searching all three familiar attractors and associated hazard points
within **1 km of the residence/IPP** by 1200. The four 15-hectare zones
total 60 hectares; ground/canine subsegments, GPS tracks and gaps are coordinated
in assignments, not presumed searched. Downwind priorities, short canine
work/cooling cycles and tactical instructions stay in assignments/safety.
Each observation pair alternates watcher/rest roles in adjacent shade while
retaining buddy contact and uninterrupted observation; no searchers are borrowed
from overlapping tasks. Task timing, actual coverage and
canine welfare require reassessment. The single illustrative **155.160 MHz VHF
simplex analog FM** channel is linked by identity to T-cards, ICS 204/SAR contacts
and the generated ICS 205A directory. No aircraft are assigned.
Each SAR task and the primary ICS 204 have two named contacts: **IC Avery Wren**
at the IC T-card's fictional phone **202-555-0101**, and **Ops Morgan Vale**
on **MR SAR 1 / 155.160 MHz**, linked to the ICS 205 channel by identity.

ICS 215A and ICS 208 carry linked tick, fall and heat mitigations. The fictional
ground-only human medical plan includes the **unstaffed Base aid-supply cache**,
a simulated ambulance service, a community hospital and an additional **fictional
regional trauma center** marked as a trauma center. MEDL coordinates from ICP;
the cache is not an extra staffed resource. Ground extraction and the ambulance
rendezvous at staging are arranged by Ops/MEDL, distinct from the Base cache.

The **active adapted ICS 206CANINE is included in the IAP**. Existing **MR-P25
Zion Clover**, K2's **Leader/Handler**, is also the explicitly linked **onsite
DVM**; their personnel card carries the fictional credential and notes. They
remain tasked on K2, **not an extra person or dedicated veterinary post**:
Ops must release or retask them before veterinary assistance. There is **no
canine aid station or canine transport service** (both free-text fields and
structured transport entries remain empty). The emergency process is limited
to **canine first aid within training, contact Ops, Ops contacts the DVM, and
Ops arranges care and transport**. Two fictional **24-hour** receiving facilities,
Cedar Vale Emergency Veterinary Hospital and Oak Hollow Veterinary Emergency
Center, have stable identities and fictional 555 numbers. Their **25- and
45-mile** distances from staging are stored as **40,233.6 and 72,420.48 meters**
and explicitly labeled hypothetical exercise assumptions, **not real route
measurements, verified availability or operational medical advice**.

The enabled ICS 233-CG action tracker uses actual item/POC/briefing/start/target/
status fields for **first tick checks for ALL teams by 1000 (Safety), hourly
hydration/electrolyte checks and Base water resupply by 0900 (Planning/Safety),
mandatory Base heat rehab with shaded rest and heat-illness checks from 1300
(Safety/MEDL; Ops staggers search-team returns), and
end-shift tick/hydration demobilization preparations from 1830 (Safety)**;
observation buddies alternate local shaded rest **1300–1320 and 1320–1340**,
maintaining buddy contact and uninterrupted observation. Ops must arrange
qualified relief **before post pairs travel to Base**; never send both observers
away and leave a post unstaffed. Rim posts remain staffed through 1900.
Blank completion dates intentionally
mean these future reminders are still open.

### Rebuild and verify

The reproducible builder is retained in
`src/test/java/org/sarmanagement/icsforms/examples/MapleRidgeExampleTest.java`.
It constructs model objects and exports through `FileIncidentStore.exportFile`;
the JSON was not hand-authored.

```sh
# Regenerate deliberately after changing the builder:
mvn -Dtest=MapleRidgeExampleTest#fixtureIsTheReproducibleStoreExport \
    -DmapleRidge.regenerate=true test

# Strict store import, content/link/count assertions, reproducibility, individual
# included-form PDFs and merged IAP rendering:
mvn -Dtest=MapleRidgeExampleTest test
```

Tests create and clean their work directories under `target/`, not the user's
incident store. The checked-in example retains deterministic incident, channel,
assignment, objective and resource identities; import gives it a new local
incident identity.
